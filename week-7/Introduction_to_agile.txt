# ITPM – Introduction to Agile

## Key Insights

- Learned Agile fundamentals and iterative software development.
- Understood Agile development phases from planning to maintenance.
- Studied the Agile Manifesto (4 values & 12 principles).
- Explored Scrum framework, including roles, events, and artifacts.
- Learned Kanban for workflow visualization and continuous improvement.
- Understood Extreme Programming (XP) practices like Pair Programming, TDD, and Continuous Integration.
- Compared Agile, Waterfall, and Hybrid project management approaches.
- Identified the benefits and challenges of Hybrid Project Management.
- Gained knowledge of Agile tools such as Jira, Trello, Git, and GitHub.
